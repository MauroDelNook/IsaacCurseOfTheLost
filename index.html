👁️How to Use

➤ Start from the center cell — that's your spawn room. Click to mark, click again to clear.

➤ Use arrow buttons to shift the whole map if your starting position was off.

➤ Mark confirmed empty spots with ✕ — it improves Delirium detection accuracy.

➤ Special room shapes (2×2, L-shaped, narrow) aren't supported — mark each cell individually.

➤ Enable Secret Rooms to highlight likely Secret / Super Secret Room spots. Click a room's edge strips to cross out walls you've already checked.

➤ Enable Fragmented Card if you have this trinket — a second Secret Room exists on this floor.

➤ Enable Luna if this floor has its effect — one extra Secret Room and one extra Super Secret Room exist. Stacks with Fragmented Card for up to 3 Secret Rooms.

➤ Enable Ultra Secret Room to find isolated spots reachable only via Red Rooms. Mark with ❓ once found.

➤ Mark potential Red Rooms to plan which wall to open — if the Red Room is adjacent to the USR, its door opens automatically when you enter.
